Federal installments are shown as four equal parts and California as 30%, 40%, 0% and 30% of the annual amounts you enter. Due dates move past weekends and the January federal holiday, but not other holidays (the April date can shift by a day or two), so confirm each date. Payments are the ledger entries you record as federal or California estimated tax, matched by date; status compares the running total paid with the running total due.
